From inspiration to comparison

Three moves. A clearer decision.

A useful Wheebuy shopping guide helps you ask better questions about a product before adding it to your shortlist.

01

Find the right starting point

Use Wheebuy finds as inspiration, then narrow your search by product type and intended use.

  • Choose everyday shoes or a specific silhouette.
  • Separate warm layers from lightweight clothing.
  • Identify the accessory that completes your outfit.
02

Read beyond the picture

A list of Wheebuy product links is only a starting point. Open each listing and compare its actual details.

  • Check the selected color, material and variant.
  • Read the item-specific measurement chart.
  • Look for clear seller and return information.
03

Build a practical shortlist

Keep a few comparable options and record the information you would need to make a confident decision.

  • Save the listing URL and date checked.
  • Record price and currency together.
  • Keep delivery estimates separate from item cost.

Before you browse

A few useful answers.

Clear expectations make a product-link directory more useful. Start with destination, fit and total cost.

What is a Wheebuy spreadsheet?

In shopping searches, a Wheebuy spreadsheet generally means a product-link list used to organize fashion finds. This page is an independent guide and category directory, rather than an official Wheebuy service or a downloadable spreadsheet.

Where do the product browsing links go?

All browsing links on this page lead to CAFANS, including its shoes, clothing and accessories category pages. They do not open a Wheebuy checkout.

How should I compare products from a spreadsheet?

Compare the exact product variant, item measurements, materials, seller details, available photos and return terms. Record the date and currency of each price, and assess shipping and other applicable charges separately.

Can a spreadsheet verify branded product authenticity?

No. A spreadsheet entry, brand name, product photo or quality-control image does not independently establish authenticity. Review verifiable provenance and the seller's supporting documentation.

How do I choose a clothing or shoe size?

Use the size chart for the exact listing. Compare clothing measurements with a similar item you own, and compare foot length with the listing's shoe chart. A familiar size label alone may not predict fit.

Does a listed price include international delivery?

Do not assume it does. Check whether the quote includes domestic delivery, international shipping, service charges and any applicable taxes or duties. Costs depend on the listing, destination and checkout terms.
